About Us

Company Overview

Estate1 Builders and Developers, operating under the umbrella of Estate 1 Holdings, bridges a prestigious real estate legacy dating back to 1970 with modern property technology. Rooted in the historical Panjabi Saudagran legacy, the enterprise has a proven track record of successfully delivering over 2,000 housing units with full possession and lease documentation across seven developmental phases.

Today, Estate 1 represents a new vanguard of corporate real estate, offering transparent transactions, reliable construction, and secure investment opportunities for both local homebuyers and overseas Pakistanis.

02
Leadership

Leadership & Corporate Vision

Under the strategic direction of Managing Director Muhammad Usman Rizwan, alongside key figures like CEO Rizwan-ur-Rehman and corporate partner Estate 1 has evolved into a multifaceted holding company.

Our leadership integrates deep technical engineering expertise, rigorous corporate governance, and a commitment to regulatory compliance as active members of the Association of Builders and Developers of Pakistan (ABAD).
