FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear answers to common questions about buying, investing, and owning property with Estate 1 in Karachi.

Still have a question? Talk to our team
Can overseas Pakistanis safely invest with Estate 1?

Yes. We cater specifically to overseas Pakistanis and digitally native homebuyers by providing a transparent, tech-enabled process. We integrate with leading PropTech platforms to ensure you get verified, upfront information, making remote investment highly secure and predictable.

Are your real estate projects legally approved?

Absolutely. Estate 1 Holdings is a formally recognized and active member of the Association of Builders and Developers of Pakistan (ABAD). We ensure all our projects, including our flagship developments, have complete lease documentation and full legal compliance, giving buyers complete peace of mind.

Do you offer flexible payment plans?

Yes. We understand the current economic environment and structure builder-backed financing to help buyers. For our flagship project, City Comfort, we offer a highly accessible 4.5-year (54-month) easy installment plan, allowing you to secure a luxury asset with manageable payments.

Are there hidden fees or surprise brokerage costs?

No. Estate 1 was built on a philosophy of transparent transacting. We guarantee honest, unmanipulated pricing and upfront information about hyper-local metrics so you never encounter the hidden costs typical of unregulated brokers.

How does Estate 1 support me during the buying process?

We design the property buying journey to be a "calm" experience. Our team offers a structured, consultative approach that includes smart search discovery, side-by-side comparative analysis of properties, and pressure-free guidance from our vetted local agents.
