FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear answers to common questions about buying, investing, and owning property in Dubai.

Still have a question? Talk to our team
Can foreigners buy property in Dubai?

Yes. Foreign nationals can purchase freehold property in designated areas across Dubai with full ownership rights.

What is an off-plan property?

An off-plan property is purchased before construction is completed, often with flexible payment plans and attractive launch pricing.

What additional costs should a buyer expect?

Typical costs may include the Dubai Land Department fee, registration charges, agency fees, and applicable mortgage expenses.

Can a property investment qualify me for a UAE residency visa?

Eligible property investments can qualify for residency options, subject to the current investment threshold and government requirements.

How does Estate1 support me during the buying process?

Our team assists with project selection, market comparisons, documentation, negotiations, and after-sales support through every stage.
