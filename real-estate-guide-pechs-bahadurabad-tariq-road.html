The Ultimate 2026 Buyer’s Guide to Real Estate in PECHS, Bahadurabad, and Tariq Road

Estate 1 Advisory Team Karachi Real Estate Research

The central arteries of Karachi remain the most resilient and highly coveted real estate micro-markets in the city. Neighborhoods like PECHS, Bahadurabad, and Tariq Road offer a unique combination of residential tranquility and high-velocity commercial activity. Proximity to Shahrah-e-Faisal and mature civic infrastructure makes these areas the ultimate destination for upwardly mobile professionals and upgrading families.

Currently, average house prices in PECHS Block 2 are hovering around PKR 15.1 Crore, while Tariq Road offers excellent commercial flats starting from PKR 1.4 Crore. When buying in these established communities, buyers must carefully weigh the distinct benefits of horizontal residential portions (which offer larger spaces and autonomous maintenance) against traditional vertical apartments (which offer top-tier security and turnkey convenience).
