The Safe Acquisition Playbook: SBCA Compliance, FBR Taxes, and Roshan Digital Accounts

Estate 1 Advisory Team Karachi Real Estate Research

Acquiring real estate in Karachi today requires strict adherence to evolving legal and financial regulations. First and foremost, buyers must ensure their chosen project has a valid No Objection Certificate (NOC) and approved building plans from the Sindh Building Control Authority (SBCA). Purchasing without verifying these approvals risks severe legal complications down the line.

Financially, the Federal Board of Revenue (FBR) heavily favors active tax filers. Under Section 236K, filers pay a 3% advance tax on property purchases, while non-filers are hit with a steep 12% penalty. For Non-Resident Pakistanis (NRPs), the State Bank’s Roshan Digital Account (RDA) has completely revolutionized the investment process. Through the Roshan Apna Ghar scheme, NRPs can secure up to 99% financing, enjoy simplified tax regulations, and easily purchase property remotely.

Whether you are a domestic buyer navigating SBCA bylaws or an overseas Pakistani deploying RDA funds, using a trusted platform is essential. Estate 1 bridges this gap by offering exclusively verified listings, ensuring that buyers can safely align their investments with legally compliant properties.
